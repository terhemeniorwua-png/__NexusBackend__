# Account deletion and workspace lifecycle

**Product decision:** approved scheduled deletion with a 30-day recovery period. **Legal approval:** outstanding; this document is not legal advice or evidence of regulatory compliance.

## Request and recovery

`POST /auth/delete` verifies the current password and account email, records `deletionRequestedAt`, sets `PENDING_DELETION`, and computes `scheduledDeletionAt` as exactly 30 days later on the server. Sessions and unused authentication tokens are revoked immediately. The user is emailed the deadline. Existing subscription state remains in place; provider cancellation is queued for the deadline to prevent future renewals.

`POST /auth/reactivate` verifies the account credentials and conditionally clears the deletion schedule only while the deadline is in the future. Recovery and finalization lock/update the same account state so only one can win. Old sessions remain revoked; the user signs in again to establish a new session. Recovery restores only workspaces marked as suspended by that deletion request and cancels pending provider cancellation. The billing cancellation worker rechecks the current workspace owner and elapsed deletion deadline immediately before the provider call; stale work for a recovered account or a workspace whose ownership changed is a no-op. Billing restoration must check provider state; recovery does not issue refunds or alter outstanding invoices.

## Workspaces and shared content

Owned workspaces are retained. Workspaces without an accepted ownership transfer are suspended; their projects, teams, memberships, messages, comments, files, and history are not deleted. A transfer is initiated by the current owner to an active workspace member. The recipient must accept. When a paid subscription exists, the recipient must separately accept billing responsibility; until then the workspace remains suspended and financial liability stays with the prior billing owner. Acceptance and billing acceptance are audited. An administrator may assign an owner after the recovery deadline through a privileged workflow; that workflow must be audited and cannot grant unrestricted access to private content.

At finalization, account profile identity is replaced with a neutral tombstone so foreign keys and shared attribution remain valid. Shared messages, comments, tasks, project updates, attachments and membership history are retained; authorship is shown as `Deleted user`, profile pictures/profile references are removed, and content itself is not automatically purged. This does not establish that personal information inside content has been anonymized. A privacy-request review process is required to evaluate access, correction, erasure or redaction requests while protecting other users' information and legitimate collaboration records. Legal review remains required.

Purely personal attachments are permanently deleted after the recovery period. Classification requires that the uploader and personal-workspace owner are the deleting account, there are no other workspace members, no other project owner/member or task participant, and no discussion relationship. Attachments linked to comments or tasks with discussion are conservatively retained; uploader identity alone is insufficient. Shared workspace or project files and files uploaded by other users are retained. Storage deletion is idempotent, performed after locking the account row against recovery, and the database attachment row is removed only after storage reports success. Failed storage operations are audited and leave the account pending for retry. A storage `404` during deletion is treated as already deleted. The current metadata model has no message or channel relationships; new sharing models require classifier review before release.

## Billing and financial records

Provider cancellation is idempotently queued for `scheduledDeletionAt`. Outstanding invoices remain payable. Payment references, receipts, refunds, subscription history, invoices, and audit records must be retained as applicable. The product has not approved numeric financial retention periods; legal/accounting review is required. A billing transfer is never inferred from workspace ownership alone.

## Implementation status and external blockers

The schema and migrations add deletion suspension tracking, scheduled billing cancellation, transfer state, billing owner attribution, and privacy request records. Request/recovery and the idempotent finalization worker retain workspaces and shared records. Purely personal files are deleted through the private object-storage adapter while the account row is locked, and their metadata is removed only after storage deletion succeeds. Failed storage operations are audited and keep the account pending for retry. Transfer initiation, incoming listing, recipient acceptance/rejection, explicit billing acceptance, and post-deletion administrator owner assignment have API operations. Provider sandbox verification requires Paystack secret/webhook credentials and USD enablement. Email delivery requires configured SMTP. Legal review and retention-period decisions remain outstanding.

## Privacy requests for retained collaboration content

The approved product policy is “retain and review”: retain shared collaboration records, replace deleted-author attribution with `Deleted user`, remove profile references, and do not automatically erase collaboration text. Authenticated verified users can submit access, correction, erasure, or redaction requests and view their own status/outcome. Active platform administrators can review, decide, and record outcomes. Approved comment redaction replaces selected exact text while preserving the rest of the comment and writes audit metadata without storing the removed text. This backend has no message, conversation, or channel models, so those content types cannot be redacted here. The separate frontend workflow remains outstanding. Legal counsel must confirm applicable rights, response deadlines, exceptions and appeal requirements.
